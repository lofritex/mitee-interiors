## Client Brief

The client envisioned a home that felt modern, spacious, and calming without appearing cold or overly minimal. The requirement was to create interiors that balanced functionality with warmth while maximizing natural light, ventilation, and openness across all spaces.
The client also wanted strong visual continuity between the kitchen, dining, and living areas while incorporating greenery and natural materials to make the home feel more connected to nature.

## Design Concept

The design concept was centered around “warm minimalism with natural character.” Rather than relying on excessive decorative elements, the space was designed using clean architectural lines, tactile materials, muted tones, and thoughtfully layered lighting.
Stone textures, wood finishes, soft fabrics, black metal accents, and indoor greens were carefully balanced to create depth and visual interest while maintaining a calm and uncluttered aesthetic.
A major focus of the design was to blur the connection between indoor and outdoor spaces through large openings, soft daylight, and integrated green features such as the vertical garden courtyard.

## Key Design Features

### Open Living & Dining Experience

- Double-height living space enhancing openness and spatial volume
- Neutral-toned upholstery and soft furnishings creating a calm visual palette
- Large curtains and expansive openings maximizing natural light diffusion
- Stone-clad feature wall adding texture and architectural depth to the living area
- Minimal furniture layout maintaining visual openness and effortless circulation

### Kitchen Design

- Contemporary kitchen with a strong balance of warmth and contrast
- Deep green handmade-style tiled backsplash acting as the focal element
- Combination of dark wood base cabinetry and light upper shutters for visual balance
- Black metal open shelving introducing an industrial-modern character
- Integrated dining island functioning as both a social and utility space
- Minimal black fixtures and hardware maintaining design consistency throughout

### Indoor-Outdoor Connection

- Vertical garden wall designed as a visual extension of the interiors
- Sculptural mirror feature within the green wall enhancing depth and reflection
- Natural stone wash basin detail creating an organic focal point
- Large black-framed openings strengthening visual continuity with outdoor greens
- Soft daylight intentionally used as a key design element across spaces

### Material & Texture Palette

- Warm wood finishes paired with neutral surfaces
- Natural stone textures used as feature elements
- Matte black metal accents creating subtle contrast
- Soft linen curtains enhancing warmth and softness
- Handmade-look green tiles adding richness and depth to the kitchen environment
- Minimal decorative styling allowing materials and textures to stand out naturally

### Spatial Planning

- Open-plan layout encouraging interaction between living, dining, and kitchen spaces
- Carefully zoned seating areas creating intimacy within larger volumes
- Functional circulation maintained without visual clutter
- Double-height proportions enhancing the perception of openness and lightness

### Styling & Atmosphere

- Minimal yet intentional décor styling throughout the residence
- Soft earthy tones maintaining a timeless aesthetic
- Integrated plants and natural elements bringing freshness into the interiors
- Layered lighting strategy balancing ambient, accent, and natural lighting
- Spaces designed to feel calm, breathable, and emotionally grounding

## Outcome

The final outcome is a refined contemporary home that feels warm, breathable, and deeply connected to nature. The project successfully balances modern aesthetics with everyday comfort through thoughtful material selection, natural light integration, and clean spatial planning.
Rather than relying on trends, the design focuses on timeless proportions, tactile materials, and subtle detailing, creating a residence that feels elegant, functional, and emotionally comforting at the same time.
